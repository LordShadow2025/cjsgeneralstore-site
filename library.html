About the Library

Every general store worth its creaky floorboards has a back room full of books, and this is ours. The Library is where the writing lives: poems, short stories, zines, journals, and the GenX blog, all by C.B. Barkley. Some of it is nostalgic, some of it is funny, and some of it is the kind of thing you read twice and then quietly check that the door is locked.

On the shelves

CJ's Blog is the Library's front desk: posts about growing up GenX, pop culture, music, and what's being built around the store. The GenX Media shelf points you to more from our corner of the web, and the Weather Audio button sets the mood with a little weather outside the windows while you read.

Two series in the making

Two book series are being written in this room. The Unfound Library is dark poetry and horror, about an archive that keeps what it finds. Tales from the General Store: The Back Room is liminal horror told like an old Golden Book, cozy pictures and all, until it isn't.

Play The Unfound Library: Overdue

The Library also has a game. Overdue is a free match-3 puzzle you play right in your browser: it's after hours, the returns are piling up, and you're putting lost books back where they belong. Ten shelves, an original soundtrack, and a story that's part of the Unfound Library's lore. It starts pleasant. It doesn't stay that way.

Something is a bit off

Regulars say the Library rearranges itself after closing. Books turn up a shelf away from where they were left, and sometimes the reading lamps are on before anyone comes in. If you look closely at the room, you'll find a keyhole. It leads to the LoreKeeper Room, where some works are shelved and some are kept. Knock first.
