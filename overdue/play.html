A pleasant lost game where something is wrong.

From The Unfound Library by C.B. Barkley

The archive is closed for the night.

Best with sound on. On an iPhone, check that the silent switch is off.

Shelves

Swap two neighboring pieces to line up three or more. Tap one piece and then its neighbor, or drag a piece toward the neighbor. Your lantern follows the pointer. On a keyboard, arrow keys move, Enter picks up a piece, and an arrow key swaps it. H shows a move and M turns sound on or off. A swap that makes no match slides back and costs no move.

Power-ups charge as you match certain pieces: Lanterns charge the Lantern Flare (clears a 3×3 patch, cubbies included), Crystals charge the Crystal Lens (one free swap anywhere, match or not), and Gears charge the Hourglass (three more moves). They don't cost a move to use.

Later shelves add dust (any match beside it sweeps it), chained locks (two Key matches), clocks (match Gears beside them), and a crack in the wall worth finding.

From Shelf II, some Tomes are locked in their cubbies. Locked pieces can't be moved, and things fall past them. Match Keys right beside a lock to open it; the Tome inside counts toward your goal.

Line up four to make a charged piece, marked with a glowing bar. When it's matched, it clears its whole row, or its whole column if you made it from a vertical line. Line up five, or make an L or T shape of five, to get an Unfound Book. Swap the book with any neighbor to clear every piece of that kind from the shelf. Two books swapped together clear everything.
