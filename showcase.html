About the Showcase

Every store has a spot by the front window where the best stuff goes. That's the Showcase. It's where the store points its spotlight at things worth your time, made by people who deserve more ears and eyes than the algorithm gives them.

Indie bands, nerd bands, and GenX artists

The Showcase features independent musicians and creators: garage bands, nerd-rock and chiptune acts, synth projects, and artists who grew up on the same mixtapes and Saturday mornings we did. Each spotlight shares who they are, what they make, and where to support them, because buying a record or a print straight from an artist still matters.

Honest reviews of odd finds

Things pass through the General Store: collectibles, gadgets, retro gear, and the occasional item nobody can identify. When something earns it, it gets a proper review here. What it is, what it's worth, whether it holds up, and whether you should go looking for one.
