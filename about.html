About CJ's General Store

CJ's General Store and More is a nostalgic journey back to simpler times — a curated space for music lovers, dreamers, creators, and collectors. Built by a Gen X'er for everyone who remembers Saturday mornings, cracked sidewalks, rushing home before those Streetlights come on.

Whether you're browsing the Sound Room, flipping through the Library, hanging in the Clubhouse, or just chilling at the Office, every corner is made with care. This isn't just a website — it's a time capsule.

Powered by passion, and constantly evolving. Welcome home.

Who's Behind the Counter

Hey, I'm C.B. Barkley. I'm a 50-year-old GenX'er, which means I grew up on Saturday morning cartoons, mixtapes recorded off the radio, quarters in the arcade, and one rule above all others: be home when the streetlights come on. Nobody tracked us. We rode our bikes until dark, and somehow we always made it back.

I built CJ's General Store for one reason: to bring people together. Somewhere along the way the internet got loud and lonely at the same time. I wanted a place that feels like the old neighborhood store, where you can wander in, find something cool, swap a story or two, and hang out a while. No algorithm, no pressure. Just good company.

Where the Stories Live

All of my ideas and stories live in the Library. That's where you'll find my poems, short stories, journals, and the GenX blog, plus the beginnings of two book series: The Unfound Library and Tales from the General Store: The Back Room. It's also home to my first game, The Unfound Library: Overdue. Fair warning, the Library is a bit off. That's on purpose.

The Rest of the Store

The Sound Room has my music, the Clubhouse is where we hang out with cartoons, MST3K, Bob Ross, and 80s and 90s radio, and the Showcase puts a spotlight on indie bands, nerd bands, and GenX artists. For the collectors, the Sentinel tools help you spot a scalper before you overpay.

Pull Up a Chair

This isn't a company. It's just me, a lot of late nights, and a lot of love for the decades that made us. I'm here to have a good time and share stories of growing up GenX, and I'd love to hear yours too. Drop me a line, come find the Discord in the Clubhouse, and stick around. The store's always open.
