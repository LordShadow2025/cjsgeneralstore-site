We Are All on the Same Blue Ball

— C.B. Barkley —

We argue.
We fight.

We divide ourselves by color, class, belief, and border.
But the truth is simple, and it does not care about our illusions:

We are all stuck on the same blue ball.

One rock. Spinning in the black.
No reset button. No escape plan.
Nowhere else to go.

We breathe the same air.
We bleed the same red.
We live under one sun and sleep beneath the same stars.

So what are we doing?

Why do we burn each other down when the fire is already rising around us?
Why poison the soil that feeds us, the water that sustains us, the sky we all share?
Why waste this chance — this brief, breathtaking chance — to get it right?

Enough with the nonsense.
Enough with the greed, the hate, the noise.

It is time to remember:

Compassion is not weakness — it is wisdom.
Unity is not surrendering — it is survival.
And peace is not a dream — it is a choice we make, or a future we lose.

We are not strangers.
We are not enemies.
We are a single species, on a fragile world, holding hands with the void.

Wake up.

Look around. This is our only home.

Start acting like it.

— C.B. Barkley
