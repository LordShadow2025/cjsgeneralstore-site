The Unfound Library

Overdue: A Shelf-by-Shelf Guide

By C.B. Barkley · October 11, 2026 · 3 min read

Stuck on a shelf in The Unfound Library: Overdue? Here's what each of the ten shelves in Chapter One asks of you, with tips for getting through them and a few hints for finding the torn pages. No major spoilers, just the help a good librarian would give you.

The basics

Swap two neighboring pieces to line up three or more of a kind. Every shelf asks you to recover a number of Tomes before your moves run out, and some shelves add extra goals, like opening locks or sweeping dust. Your remaining goals are always shown at the top of the board.

  • Charged pieces: line up four of a kind to make one. Match it again and it fires a beam across its row or down its column.
  • Unfound Books: line up five in a row, or make an L or T shape of five. Swap the Book with any piece to take every piece of that kind off the shelf.
  • Candles: finish with moves to spare to earn up to three candles. Your best rating for each shelf is saved in the Menu.

Shelf I: Sorting the Shelves

A gentle start. Look for matches near the bottom of the board, because clearing low pieces makes everything above them fall, and falling pieces set off free cascades.

Shelf II: The Locked Cubbies

Four Tomes are locked behind iron bars. Any Key that clears right next to a lock opens it, and the Tome inside counts toward your goal. Keys show up more often on this shelf, so be patient and set up Key matches beside each lock rather than chasing Tomes.

Shelf III: Dust Sheets

Any match right beside a dusty cubby sweeps it, and thick dust takes two sweeps. This is also where the first power-up arrives: match Lanterns to charge the Lantern Flare, which burns away a three-by-three patch of the shelf, dust included. Save it for dust stuck in a corner.

Shelf IV: The Crack in the Wall

There's a faint violet crack in the back of the shelf. Make a match that touches it and something comes out of the wall, along with three extra moves. It's optional, but you'll want it.

Shelf V: Borrowed Light

You'll need to make three charged pieces. Look for spots where one swap lines up four. The Crystal Lens arrives here: match Crystals to charge it, and it lets you make one swap anywhere, even one that doesn't make a match. It's perfect for setting up a line of four.

Shelf VI: The Unfound Book

Make an Unfound Book, and open a chained lock and a regular one. Chains need two Key matches beside them. An L or T shape is often easier to spot than a straight line of five, so watch corners where two lines of three meet. Tip: a charged beam that crosses a locked or chained cubby counts as a hit too.

Shelf VII: Resonance

Every so often a music box starts to sing, and the shelf glows violet. Notes matched while it's singing count toward your goal. Line up a Note match and wait for the glow before you swap. The Hourglass arrives here too: match Gears to charge it for three extra moves.

Shelf VIII: Static

The lights flicker on this shelf, and you may see something in the static. It never hides your pieces for long, and the "See it again" button replays it if you missed it. Locks and dust return here, so split your attention between Keys and sweeping.

Shelf IX: The Clockwork Shelf

Old clocks fill five cubbies, running backward. Match Gears right beside a clock to stop it. Gears show up more often here, and every Gear you match also charges the Hourglass, so this shelf tends to reward patience.

Shelf X: The Library Speaks

Everything you've learned, on one shelf: locks, dust, and clocks, plus a big Tome goal. Use power-ups early on the hardest cubbies rather than saving them. And listen. The Library has a few things to say.

Finding the torn pages

From Shelf IV onward, every shelf hides a crack in the wall. Look for a thin violet line that glows faintly, usually somewhere near the middle of the board. A match that touches it pulls out a torn page. Found pages are kept in the Torn Pages room, which you can open from the Menu or the title screen, and the ones you're missing are marked, so you'll know which shelves to revisit.
