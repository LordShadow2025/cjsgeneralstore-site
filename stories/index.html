Stories

From the porch of CJ's General Store · by C.B. Barkley

Pull up a chair. This is where the store keeps its stories: what it was like growing up GenX, the things we loved and lost along the way, the artists worth your time, and the strange goings-on in the Unfound Library behind the store.

Everything here is written for the people who remember being home when the streetlights came on, and for anyone who wishes they'd been there. New stories are added to the shelves as they're written. If one of them brings back a memory of your own, that's the whole point.
