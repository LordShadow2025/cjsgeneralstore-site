About the Sound Room

The Sound Room is the musical side of the writing. Everything here is by C.B. Barkley: ambient pieces for late nights, synth tracks that sound like a drive past closed shops and streetlights, and lyrical songs that started life as poems. If the Library is where the words live, this is where they hum.

Start here: Streetlight Memories

The player in this room is loaded with Streetlight Memories. Give it a listen, then head to Bandcamp for the full collection, where you can stream, buy, and support the music directly.

Music for The Unfound Library: Overdue

The Sound Room also scored a game. Overdue, the free puzzle game in the Library, has a soundtrack written for it, from the title song The Unfound Library and the quiet The Door Behind the Store to the cosmic chaos of The Static Room, The Clock Runs Backward, and The Library Speaks. Play it with the sound on.

Watch and listen

The YouTube channel has music videos and ambient sessions, good for working, reading, or just leaving on in the background.

Words behind the music

Many of these songs began as written pieces. If a lyric sticks with you, wander over to the Library, where the poems and stories they grew from are shelved. New singles and drops show up in this room first.
